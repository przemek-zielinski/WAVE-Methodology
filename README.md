## The Problem

Every organization now has access to AI. Almost no one has a coherent methodology for working with it.

Prompt engineering teaches how to write better requests — but a good request without structured preparation is a precise question asked in the wrong context. AI courses are tool manuals. Responsible AI guidelines are regulatory frameworks. None of them answer the fundamental question: **how should a human expert systematically collaborate with AI to produce results neither could achieve alone?**

WAVE answers that question.

---

## The Solution in 30 Seconds

WAVE organizes human-AI collaboration into three layers built on one principle:

> **70% human preparation → 30% AI execution → 10x the result**

```
HUMAN EXPERTISE (70%)                AI AMPLIFICATION (30%)
┌──────────────────────────┐         ┌──────────────────────────┐
│ Domain knowledge          │         │ Data processing          │
│ Situational context       │────────→│ Pattern recognition      │
│ Experience & judgment     │         │ Variant generation       │
│ Responsibility            │←────────│ Scale & speed            │
└──────────────────────────┘         └──────────────────────────┘

Layer 1: DataPrep        — Organize your domain knowledge
Layer 2: Prompt2Data     — Create precise, bounded tasks for AI
Layer 3: Prompt2Prompt   — Build reusable meta-prompts that generate prompts
```

## Who Is WAVE For?

**For anyone who works with AI** — not just developers.

WAVE was born in software engineering and has been fully implemented there. But its three layers contain nothing specific to code. They describe the universal relationship between human expertise and AI capability.

If you are a **developer**, a **pharmacist**, a **doctor**, a **lawyer**, a **teacher**, an **engineer**, an **NGO worker**, a **manager**, or anyone else who collaborates with AI in your professional work — WAVE is designed for you.

## Quick Start

**Step 1:** Read the [Core Methodology](docs/WAVE_Methodology_v1.0_EN.md) (20 min)

**Step 2:** If you work in software, read the [SE Applied Guide](docs/WAVE_Software_Engineering_v1.0_EN.md) (20 min)

**Step 3:** Try WAVE on your next task:
1. Before asking AI anything, spend 70% of your time organizing what you know (DataPrep)
2. Write one precise, bounded task with all context included (Prompt2Data)
3. Evaluate the result against your success criteria
4. Notice the difference

**Step 4:** Tell us what happened — [open an Issue](../../issues) or start a [Discussion](../../discussions)

## The Lean Precedent

Lean started in Toyota's factories in the 1950s. Today it saves lives in hospitals, improves schools, and transforms governments. The journey from "Lean Manufacturing" to "Lean Thinking" took decades — partly because the original label made non-manufacturers say "that's not for us."

WAVE launches as a generic methodology from day one. Software engineering is the first proof point, not the scope boundary. The three layers speak about human expertise and AI — not about code.

## Origins

WAVE was created in January 2026 by **Przemysław Zieliński** during the development of [IDareU](https://idareu.io) — a gamified learning platform. Working as a solo founder with AI as his primary collaborator, Przemek discovered that the bottleneck was never the AI's intelligence — it was human preparation. Structuring that discovery into a repeatable system produced WAVE.

The methodology was documented with **Claude** (Anthropic) in an intensive collaborative session and refined over the following weeks through practical application.

WAVE is published as open-source because Przemek believes the methodology for working with AI should be a shared resource for humanity, not a proprietary advantage.

# Contributing to WAVE / Współtworzenie WAVE

Thank you for your interest in WAVE. This methodology grows when people use it, question it, and improve it.

Dziękujemy za zainteresowanie metodyką WAVE. Rośnie ona gdy ludzie jej używają, kwestionują ją i ulepszają.

---

## 🔍 Looking for Co-Maintainers

WAVE was created by Przemysław Zieliński during the development of IDareU. The methodology is open-source and needs dedicated co-maintainers to grow it beyond its origins.

**What co-maintainers do:**
- Review and merge Pull Requests
- Triage Issues and respond to community questions
- Guide the methodology's evolution across domains
- Help maintain quality and consistency of documentation

**What we are looking for:**
- Passion for human-AI collaboration
- Experience working with AI in any professional domain
- Ability to commit a few hours per week
- Constructive, respectful communication

**How to apply:** Open an Issue titled **"Co-Maintainer Application"** and tell us about yourself — your domain, your experience with AI, and why WAVE resonates with you.

## How to Contribute

### 1. Test WAVE in Your Domain

The most valuable contribution is a real-world test. Apply WAVE to your work — whether that is software, healthcare, education, law, pharmacy, engineering, NGO, or anything else — and share what happened.

**How:** Open an Issue titled **"Field Report: WAVE in [Your Domain]"** describing:
- What domain you work in
- How you applied the three layers (DataPrep, Prompt2Data, Prompt2Prompt)
- What worked well
- What did not work or needed adaptation
- Any metrics you collected

### 2. Write an Applied Guide

WAVE currently has one deep applied guide (Software Engineering). We need more.

If you have tested WAVE in your field and want to write a full applied guide, follow the structure of `docs/WAVE_Software_Engineering_v1.0_EN.md` adapted to your domain. Submit a Pull Request to `docs/`.

Domains we are especially interested in:
- Healthcare & clinical diagnostics
- Education & personalized learning
- Law & case analysis
- Pharmaceutical research & drug discovery
- NGO & social impact planning
- Engineering & design optimization

### 3. Improve Existing Documentation

Found an error? A confusing section? A better way to explain something? Open a Pull Request with your improvement.

**Guidelines:**
- Keep language clear, direct, and free of unnecessary jargon
- Maintain the principle: generic core stays generic, applied guides go deep in their domain
- If editing the Polish version, use natural Polish — no corporate-speak, no unnecessary anglicisms
- If editing the English version, keep it accessible to non-native speakers

### 4. Create Templates

WAVE v1.1 will include practical templates for DataPrep, Prompt2Data, and Prompt2Prompt. If you want to contribute templates — especially domain-specific ones — submit a Pull Request to `templates/`.

Each template should include:
- Generic structure (domain-agnostic)
- At least one filled example from your domain
- Brief instructions for adaptation

### 5. Translate

WAVE currently exists in English and Polish. We welcome translations to any language. If you want to translate, open an Issue first to coordinate and avoid duplicate work.

### 6. Constructive Criticism

We actively seek people who will break WAVE — find its weaknesses, contradictions, and blind spots. If you believe a layer is unnecessary, a metric is misleading, or the 70/30 ratio is wrong for your domain — tell us. Open an Issue titled **"Criticism: [Topic]"** with a clear argument.

The only thing we ask: be specific and constructive. "This doesn't work" without context is not actionable. "Layer 2 fails in emergency medicine because time constraints prevent structured DataPrep — here is what I tried" is invaluable.

## Code of Conduct

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Focus on ideas, not people
- Welcome newcomers — WAVE is for everyone who works with AI
- Disagree openly but kindly

---

## Technical Guidelines

- All documentation in Markdown (.md)
- File naming: `WAVE_[Topic]_v[X.Y]_[LANG].md` (e.g., `WAVE_Healthcare_v1.0_EN.md`)
- One document per applied domain
- Diagrams in Mermaid or ASCII (renders everywhere)
- No external dependencies for viewing documentation
